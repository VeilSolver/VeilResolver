HOW IT WORKS
Three steps. Zero leaks.
01
Build & Encrypt
Intent encrypted ECIES client-side with the solver's enclave public key. The solver never sees plaintext — not even in transit.
02
TEE Inference
AI computes the execution plan inside an Intel TDX enclave on 0G Compute. Strategy logic is private by hardware. Host OS cannot read enclave memory.
03
Atomic Settlement
Plan verified via ECDSA on 0G Chain. minAmountOut enforced by VeilSolver.sol. Replay protection via onchain intent mapping.
